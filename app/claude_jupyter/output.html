# Marathon Race Day Nutrition Plan

## Pre-Race (3-7 Days Before)
**Carb Loading Phase**
- **Carbs**: 10-12g per kg body weight daily = 680-816g carbs
- Focus on easily digestible carbs: pasta, rice, bread, potatoes, oats
- Maintain protein (1.6g/kg = 109g) and reduce fiber/fat slightly
- Example: 3000-3500 calories with 60-65% from carbs

## Race Day (2-3 Hours Before)
- **Meal**: 200-300g carbs, low fat, low fiber, familiar foods
  - Option 1: 2-3 bagels with peanut butter & banana
  - Option 2: Oatmeal with honey and berries
  - Option 3: Toast with jam and sports drink
- Drink 400-600ml water/electrolyte drink
- *Finish 3 hours before race start*

## During Race
- **Every 45-60 min**: 30-60g carbs per hour
  - Sports drinks, gels, energy chews, or sports bars
- **Hydration**: 400-800ml fluids per hour (adjust for heat/humidity)
- Electrolytes (sodium 300-600mg per liter)

## Post-Race Recovery
- Within 30 mins: 1.2g carbs/kg + 0.3g protein/kg = 81g carbs + 20g protein
  - Chocolate milk, banana with yogurt, or recovery drink

## Key Points
✓ Practice this plan during training runs
✓ Stay hydrated consistently in days leading up
✓ Avoid anything new on race day
